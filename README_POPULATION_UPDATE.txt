MINI STRONGHOLD - POPULATION & SELECTION UPDATE

Changes:
- New game starts with 100% popularity and 24 peasants.
- Base population capacity is 24; houses and granaries still increase capacity.
- Peasants immigrate while popularity is at least 50%, up to the population cap.
- Hunger reduces popularity gradually by 4 points every 5 seconds instead of an instant large drop.
- Selected soldiers show individual selection numbers above their heads.
- HUD shows working and idle peasant counts.
- Idle peasants are represented around a small animated communal campfire beside the keep.
- Idle peasants remain available for recruitment; each recruited soldier uses one peasant as before.

Build and run:
Run RUN_GAME_ENGLISH.bat on Windows with MSYS2 UCRT64 GCC and Raylib installed at C:\\msys64\\ucrt64.
