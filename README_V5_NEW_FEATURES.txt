MINI STRONGHOLD - V5 (built on top of your "Arabic Units Balance Update" version)
==================================================================================
Run RUN_GAME_ENGLISH.bat as before.

1. CHEATS            Options > Cheats ON/OFF (next to Show FPS). When ON, press F9 (or the Menu button) for the cheat menu:
                     +gold, +materials, +food, +weapons, +30 peasants, popularity 100%, heal everything, instant build,
                     invincible army, kill all enemy soldiers, spawn 10 knights, WIN the mission.
                     A game that used a cheat does not unlock the next campaign mission. The setting is saved in settings.cfg.
2. HEALER            Military tab. Heals your soldiers inside a green circle (about 5 tiles, 7 HP per second).
                     The circle is shown on the ground: faint all the time, bright when you hover/select it or while placing it.
                     Green "+" signs rise from healed soldiers.
3. ENEMY MESSAGES    A panel on the map (bottom right) with the lord's animated portrait clip (scanlines, talking bob, sound waves),
                     a typed message and a talking voice (different pitch for every lord) + a scroll sting.
                     Messages come at the start of a mission, when a lord attacks, when his castle is finished,
                     when he is defeated, and as taunts every 100-180 seconds.
4. BUILDING ANIMATION Every building is now a construction site: foundation -> scaffolding -> the building rises from the ground
                     -> scaffolding removed -> finished (with builders swinging tools, hammer sounds and dust).
                     Unfinished buildings do not work (no production, training, houses, towers...). Enemy lords' castles are built the same way.
5. VICTORY           Animated "VICTORY!" logo (medal, crossed swords, laurels, ribbon, confetti) + a voice saying "Victory!".
                     On Windows the game renders the word once with the PC's own speech voice (assets/sfx/victory_voice_sapi.wav);
                     if that is not possible it uses the built-in synthesized voice assets/sfx/victory_voice.wav.
6. HUNTING           Wild deer, rabbits and boars roam the map. Hunter's Hut (Farms tab, needs 1 peasant): the hunter walks out,
                     shoots an animal, picks up the kill, carries it home and butchers it into MEAT (deer 12, rabbit 5, boar 16).
                     Meat is food (eaten first) and counts as a food type for popularity. It is shown in the top bar and the Advisor (F1).

Sounds: assets/sfx/msg.wav, voice.wav, victory_voice.wav, hammer.wav, heal.wav, built.wav (all generated for this game).
