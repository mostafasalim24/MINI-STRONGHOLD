STRONGHOLD - UNIT ROSTER & BALANCE UPDATE

NEW / UPDATED UNITS
- Longbowman and Pikeman added to the medieval Barracks roster.
- Arab Swordsman and Arab Guard added to the Arabic War Tent roster.
- Camel Archer added: 85 HP, 18 damage, 165 range; stronger than the Horse Archer and costs 100 gold.
- Demolisher added to the Siege Workshop roster as a heavy siege attacker.

TRAINING RULES
- Barracks now has 8 medieval unit choices. These require the matching bow, spear or sword stock plus gold. Recruitment prices were reduced.
- War Tent now has 8 Arabic/desert unit choices and uses gold only; it does not consume weapon stock.
- Siege Workshop trains Catapults, Battering Rams and Demolishers.
- Each recruited unit still requires one available peasant.

BUILDING BALANCE
- Construction material costs are capped at 20 wood and 50 stone.
- War Tent construction costs 90 gold only.

NOTES
- Source and assets are included. Run RUN_GAME_ENGLISH.bat on Windows with the MSYS2 UCRT64 + raylib setup described in the project.
- This environment cannot verify a Windows build or live gameplay. Please compile and test on the target PC.
